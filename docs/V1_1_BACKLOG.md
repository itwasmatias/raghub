# Post-v1.0 Backlog

Feature work is frozen until `v1.0.0` release gates pass.

Candidates for v1.1:

- Additional sports and non-sports Situation workflows.
- Shared distributed rate limiting and job queues.
- More licensed sportsbook providers and liquidity measures.
- Richer opponent-defense and lineup interaction models.
- Automated model promotion and rollback.
- Multi-user permissions and organization workspaces.
- Mobile-specific decision views.
- Expanded research connectors and document collections.

Items belong here rather than delaying the NBA flagship release.
